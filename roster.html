The full roster: all 89 fighters, what each of their special moves does, and what they are good and bad at.

How to fight

MoveController: left stick · Keyboard: W A S D
JumpController: X or Y, or flick the stick up · Keyboard: Space. Jump again in the air.
Attack (A)Controller: A · Keyboard: J. Tap for jabs; in the air for aerials.
Tilt attackHold a direction + A, or use the right stick. Quick pokes.
Smash attackFlick a direction and press A at the same moment. Hold A to charge. These are the finishers.
Special (B)Controller: B · Keyboard: K. B alone, or with left/right, up or down, gives four different moves. Up + B is usually how you get back to the stage.
Shield and dodgeController: ZL / ZR · Keyboard: L. Hold to shield; flick a direction while shielding to roll or dodge.
GrabController: L / R, or shield + A · Keyboard: U. Then press a direction to throw.
Quirk actionShield + B. A utility move unique to each fighter; most do no damage.
Final SmashDealing and taking damage fills a meter. When it is full, press B with no direction.
How you winDamage is a percentage: the higher it is, the further a hit sends you. Knock rivals off the screen to take a stock.
